About Pet Pal

Pet Pal is a comprehensive iOS application designed to help pet owners provide the best care for their furry, feathered, and scaled companions. Whether you have a dog, cat, bird, rabbit, hamster, turtle, or fish, Pet Pal adapts to your pet's specific needs.

Our app combines intuitive tracking features with helpful guidance to ensure your pets stay healthy and happy. From daily care routines to long-term health management, Pet Pal is your trusted companion in pet parenthood.

Why Pet Pal?

  • ✓ Support for multiple pet species
  • ✓ Customizable tracking intervals
  • ✓ Offline functionality with local data storage
  • ✓ Beautiful, intuitive interface
  • ✓ Your data stays on your device

Privacy First

Your pet's data is your data. Pet Pal is built with privacy as a core principle:

📱 Local Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Core Data. Nothing is sent to external servers.

🔒 No Tracking

We don't collect, track, or share any personal information or usage data.

🚫 No Ads

Pet Pal is ad-free. No third-party advertising or analytics frameworks.

Getting Started

New to Pet Pal? Here are some quick tips:

  • Add your first pet using the + button
  • Customize food and water durations based on your pet's needs
  • Enable notifications to stay on top of care tasks
  • Tap on your pet's photo for species-specific care guidance
